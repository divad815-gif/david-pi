Storage and backups

Update recovery storage

By default, recovery snapshots stay on the library drive in a separate private folder. The first snapshot needs room for a full copy; later updates reuse unchanged snapshot copies. The latest successful recovery point is kept, along with failed or interrupted attempts. These snapshots do not protect against drive failure.
